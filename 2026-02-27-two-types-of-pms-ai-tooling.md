## 摘要

AI 工具浪潮中出现了两类 PM：第一类把 Claude Code 和 MCP 当研究助手，问答、复制粘贴，工作流程没变，只是稍快一点。第二类则把 AI 嵌入工作流程本身——让智能体自动拉数据、生成 WBR（周业务回顾）、分析竞品、写 PRD 草稿。两者的差距会随时间复利式扩大：前者快 10%，后者从根本上改变了工作方式。

## 全文

Two types of PMs are emerging from the same AI tooling wave.

The first group treats Claude Code and MCP as research assistants. They ask questions, get answers, copy-paste insights into their existing docs and slide decks. Their workflow is the same, just slightly faster.

The second group is doing something different. They're building AI into the workflow itself. They set up agents that automatically pull data, generate WBRs, analyze competitors, and draft PRDs. They're not 10% faster — they're operating in a completely different way.

Same tools. Different mindset. The gap compounds over time.

— @aakashgupta, 2026-02-25
