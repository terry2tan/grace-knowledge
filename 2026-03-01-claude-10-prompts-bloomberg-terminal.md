### 提示词 1：高盛基本面分析筛选器（Goldman Sachs Fundamental Analysis Screener）

> Act as a Goldman Sachs senior equity analyst. Analyze [COMPANY] using their proprietary GS SUSTAIN framework. Evaluate: 1) Industry positioning using Porter's Five Forces with specific market share data 2) Management quality score based on capital allocation history and ROE trends 3) ESG momentum score using Goldman's proprietary metrics 4) Free cash flow yield vs sector peers 5) Create a "conviction buy/sell" recommendation with 12-month price target. Format as an actual GS equity research note with bull/base/bear scenarios.

### 提示词 2：摩根士丹利技术分析 Dashboard（Morgan Stanley Technical Analysis Dashboard）

> You are Morgan Stanley's chief technical strategist. For [STOCK/INDEX], provide: 1) Multi-timeframe analysis (daily/weekly/monthly) using MS's proprietary AlphaWise signals 2) Identify key support/resistance using Fibonacci, pivot points, and volume profile 3) Momentum indicators: RSI divergences, MACD histogram analysis, Stochastic oscillator 4) Options market intelligence: put/call ratios, unusual options activity, implied volatility term structure 5) Generate specific entry/exit points with exact price levels and stop losses. Include MS-style risk/reward matrices.

### 提示词 3：桥水风险评估框架（Bridgewater Risk Assessment Framework）

> Channel Ray Dalio's All Weather framework. Analyze [PORTFOLIO/ASSET] through: 1) Macro environment assessment using Bridgewater's "economic machine" template - growth vs inflation quadrant 2) Correlation analysis across asset classes during different market regimes 3) Tail risk scenarios using historical analogues (1929, 2000, 2008, 2020) 4) Risk parity weighting suggestions 5) "Holy Grail" diversification score. Identify which assets perform in rising/falling growth AND rising/falling inflation environments. Format as a Bridgewater Daily Observations memo.

### 提示词 4：摩根大通盈利分析器（JPMorgan Earnings Analyzer）

> Act as JPMorgan's earnings specialist. For [COMPANY]'s upcoming earnings: 1) Reconstruct consensus estimates using JPM's proprietary earnings model template 2) Identify the 3 "variant perceptions" where Street estimates may be wrong 3) Analyze earnings quality: accruals ratio, cash conversion, one-time items 4) Model sensitivity analysis: what moves EPS by +/- 10%? 5) Historical earnings reaction analysis: average move, implied move vs actual 6) Provide specific options strategy to position for earnings. Include JPM's "earnings scorecard" format.

### 提示词 5：贝莱德股息收入分析器（BlackRock Dividend Income Analyzer）

> You are BlackRock's income portfolio manager. Analyze [STOCK/ETF] for dividend sustainability using: 1) Dividend safety score: payout ratio trends, free cash flow coverage, debt service coverage 2) DGR (Dividend Growth Rate) sustainability using BlackRock's proprietary model 3) Sector-relative yield analysis with historical percentile rankings 4) Balance sheet stress test: can dividend survive a 30% revenue decline? 5) Total return decomposition: yield vs growth vs valuation change 6) Compare to relevant iShares ETF benchmark. Create a "dividend health dashboard" with red/yellow/green indicators.

### 提示词 6：城堡对冲行业轮动策略师（Citadel Sector Rotation Strategist）

Format as a Citadel morning briefing note.
